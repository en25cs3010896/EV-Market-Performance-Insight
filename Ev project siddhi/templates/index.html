Analytical Progression

EV Market Narrative & Story

A guided visual walkthrough chronicling the chronological transition from early electric niche vehicles to mainstream adoption.

Stage 01

The Inception Phase

Early market introduction (1997 - 2011) driven by early technology adopters and hybrid pioneers, limited by sub-100-mile battery capabilities.

Stage 02

Policy & CAFV Catalysts

Implementation of Clean Alternative Fuel Vehicle (CAFV) tax rebates and state-level purchase incentives accelerating consumer awareness.

Stage 03

Mass BEV Proliferation

Exponential scaling led by Tesla Model 3 / Y, Nissan Leaf, and Chevrolet Bolt, achieving 200+ mile ranges and fast-charging network parity.

Stage 04

Competitive Horizon

Legacy automakers (Ford, BMW, Hyundai, VW) entering mainstream market segments, diversifying form factors from sedans to electric SUVs and trucks.

Academic Project Scope & Dataset

This project investigates the evolution and performance indicators of the modern Electric Vehicle (EV) market. Using empirical population data, the study explores transition dynamics from internal combustion engines (ICE) to Battery Electric (BEV) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEV).
